Biography

Gokaran Shukla is a theoretical and computational physicist who studies how electrons transport charge, spin and orbital angular momentum in quantum materials and across their interfaces.

He is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), Saudi Arabia, where he has worked since January 2022. Before joining KAUST he was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Chemistry at the Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST), Korea, in the group of Prof. Geunsik Lee.

He received his PhD in Physics from Trinity College Dublin, with a thesis on the computational design of magnetic tunnel junctions, and his B.Tech. and M.Tech. degrees in Materials Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur. His work combines density functional theory, Wannier-function tight-binding models and purpose-built GPU codes, and is carried out in close collaboration with experimental groups in spintronics.

Research interests

Spin and orbital Hall effects

Intrinsic spin and orbital Hall conductivity tensors of transition metals, oxides and two-dimensional MXenes, including the comparison of atom-centred and modern-theory orbital moments.

Spin–orbit and orbital torques

Spin and orbital Edelstein effects and unconventional torques at ferromagnet interfaces with transition-metal dichalcogenides, Weyl semimetals, kagome metals and altermagnets.

Berry curvature and nonlinear Hall transport

Berry curvature dipoles and higher-order Hall responses in low-symmetry, ferroelectric and Janus van der Waals bilayers.

Spin-polarised quantum transport

Non-equilibrium Green's function design of magnetic tunnel junctions and tunnel barriers for high spin-polarised current.

Superconductivity

Electron–phonon coupling and Migdal–Eliashberg theory for boride superconductors, and data-driven prediction of transition temperatures.

Correlated electron methods

Slave-boson approaches for correlated kagome metals and quantum algorithms for fermionic lattice models.
